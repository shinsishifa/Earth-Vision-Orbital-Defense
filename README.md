# 🌍 Earth Vision: Orbital Defense

**Earth Vision: Orbital Defense** is an arcade-style planetary defense game built natively with HTML5 Canvas and Vanilla JavaScript. Players control an orbital energy shield to deflect incoming meteor showers and orbital debris, protecting Earth's atmosphere across scaling difficulty waves.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Zero External Dependencies:** Built 100% with native HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript.
* **Glowing Orbital Mechanics:** Features 3D-styled Earth rendering, atmospheric glow, particle explosion physics, and dynamic rotation tracking.
* **Smooth Pointer Controls:** Rotate your shield intuitively using mouse movements or mobile touchscreen dragging.
* **Progressive Difficulty Curve:** Asteroid spawn rates and velocities automatically scale up as your score increases.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Deploy Shield:** Click **Deploy Shield** to begin defense protocol.
2. **Rotate Shield:** Move your mouse cursor or drag your finger around Earth to position the energy shield.
3. **Defend Earth:** Intercept falling red meteors before they hit the planet surface!
